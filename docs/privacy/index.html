Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 8 November 2026

In short. DPGlance has no accounts and no developer server, and we do not run analytics or crash reporting of our own. Your clipboard is checked on your device and never stored or sent anywhere. The only things that leave your phone are the lookup of a profile on snapchat.com and the ad requests handled by Google AdMob.

1. Who we are

DPGlance is a free Android app published by an independent developer (GitHub: soham-tejani). In this policy, “we” and “us” mean the DPGlance developer. To contact us, see Support.

DPGlance is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Snap Inc.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, camera or microphone data, or any account details. DPGlance has no sign-in. We do not operate a server that receives data from the app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

DPGlance uses Google AdMob to show rewarded ads. You always choose to start an ad. According to Google’s documentation, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention purposes:

  • your device’s IP address, which Google may use to estimate your general location;
  • product interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information about the app and SDK performance, such as launch time and hang rate; and
  • device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

DPGlance declares the advertising ID permission so ads can be served. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or limit ad personalisation, in your Android settings (usually under Google or Privacy, then Ads), and manage Google ad settings at adssettings.google.com. Where the law requires consent, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, DPGlance shows Google’s consent form first and does not request ads until consent allows it.

Google processes this data under its own terms. See Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from ads.

4. How we use information

Only to run the features you ask for: finding a link, showing a profile, saving files, and showing ads that fund the free app. We do not sell your personal information. We have no user database to sell or share.

5. Who else is involved

  • Snap Inc. receives the profile and media requests described above.
  • Google receives ad-related data through AdMob and the User Messaging Platform consent form.

We do not control these companies. Their own privacy policies apply to what they receive.

6. Retention and deletion

We hold no personal data about you. Data stored on your phone (recent profiles and the intro flag) stays until you remove it or uninstall DPGlance. Files you saved stay in your gallery until you delete them. Data held by Google or Snap is governed by their policies and controls.

7. Security

DPGlance connects only over HTTPS and blocks unencrypted traffic. Its stored data is excluded from Android backups. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we keep the amount of data handled to a minimum.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, California’s CCPA/CPRA or India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data. Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to provide or delete, but you can still contact us and we will respond. To control advertising data, use the Android and Google settings described in section 3.

9. Children

DPGlance is not directed to children under 13, and ads are not directed to children. Snapchat itself requires users to be at least 13. If you believe a child has used DPGlance in a way that concerns you, please contact us.

10. International processing

Google and Snap operate globally, so data sent to them may be processed in countries other than yours.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes, for example if we add a feature that handles data differently. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant, we will say so on this page and in the app update notes.

12. Contact

Questions, requests or concerns: open a request on GitHub using the Privacy request form. Please do not put personal details in a public issue. Tell us what you need and we will reply with a private way to continue if one is needed. More options are on the Support page.
