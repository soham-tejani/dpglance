Features

Everything a profile viewer needs, nothing it doesn’t.

Finds your link

Copy a public profile link and open DPGlance. It spots the link for you, or you can paste a link or username yourself.

Full-size photos and covers

See the profile picture and cover image at the largest size the profile offers, with pinch-to-zoom.

Highlights and Spotlight

Browse a profile’s public highlights and Spotlight rows and open each one in a full-screen viewer.

Save to your gallery

Save photos and videos to Pictures/DPGlance using Android’s media storage. No storage permission needed.

Private by design

No account, no analytics of ours and no developer server. Your recent profiles stay on your phone.

Fair, rewarded ads

One short ad that you choose to watch unlocks a profile. Nothing pops up out of the blue.

How it works

Three taps from link to photo.

  1. Copy a link

    Copy a public profile link from Snapchat, a browser or a message.

  2. Open DPGlance

    DPGlance detects the link and loads the public profile. Or paste it yourself.

  3. View and save

    Watch one short rewarded ad to unlock, then view full-size and save to your gallery.

Responsible use

Public content, creator’s rights.

  • DPGlance only shows what a profile publishes publicly.
  • Pictures belong to their creators. Save them for personal use and don’t repost without permission.
  • Don’t use DPGlance to harass, stalk or impersonate anyone.

Your privacy

What leaves your phone, in plain words.

  • Your clipboard is checked on your device when you open the app. It is never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Only the username in a link you copied is looked up on snapchat.com.
  • Ads come from Google AdMob, with Google’s consent form where the law requires it.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is DPGlance?

DPGlance is a free Android app that shows the public photos on a profile (the profile picture, cover image and highlights) at full size from a link you copy, and lets you save them to your gallery.

Does it work with private accounts?

No. DPGlance only shows what a profile publishes publicly. A personal account without a public profile photo shows a “no public photo” message. DPGlance never asks you to sign in and never shows anything that is not already public.

Do I need an account?

No. DPGlance has no accounts, no sign-in and no developer server.

Why do I watch an ad?

DPGlance is free. Unlocking a profile’s photo and cover, or a highlight, takes one short rewarded ad from Google AdMob. You always choose to start it, and nothing pops up unexpectedly. If an ad can’t load after a couple of tries, DPGlance lets you continue.

What does DPGlance do with my clipboard?

Each time you open the app, it checks on your device whether the text you most recently copied is a Snapchat profile link. Nothing from your clipboard is stored or sent anywhere, except the username in that link, which is looked up on snapchat.com. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Where are my saved files?

Photos are saved to Pictures/DPGlance/username and videos to Movies/DPGlance/username on your phone, using Android’s standard media storage, so DPGlance needs no storage permission.

Is DPGlance affiliated with Snapchat?

No. DPGlance is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Snap Inc. Snapchat and the Ghost logo are trademarks of Snap Inc.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 10 (API level 29) and later.

Who owns the pictures I see in DPGlance?

Their creators. Save images for personal use only, and don’t repost them without the creator’s permission.

How do I report misuse or ask for removal?

Open a support request on the Support page. DPGlance only reads what a profile already publishes on Snapchat, so the quickest way to stop a profile appearing is to change that profile’s visibility in Snapchat. We also review every request.
