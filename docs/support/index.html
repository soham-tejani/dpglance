Support and reports

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Contact us

The fastest way to reach us is a request on GitHub. Pick the form that fits:

  • Get help or report a bug: something is not working.
  • Report misuse or request removal: you are a creator or rights holder with a concern.
  • Privacy request: questions or requests about your data.

Please keep it private. GitHub requests are public. Do not post personal details, screenshots of private information or anything sensitive. Describe what you need and we will reply with a private way to continue if one is needed.

Common problems

DPGlance doesn’t find my link

Copy the full profile link and open DPGlance again, or paste the link or username into the box on the home screen. Only public profile links are supported.

“No public photo” or “Profile not found”

DPGlance shows only what a profile publishes publicly. A personal account without a public profile has no public photo to show. Check the username for typos.

The ad won’t load

Check your connection and try again. If an ad can’t load after a couple of tries, DPGlance lets you continue.

Where did my saved files go?

Photos are in Pictures/DPGlance/username and videos in Movies/DPGlance/username. Look in your gallery or Files app under those folders.

Something suddenly stopped working

DPGlance relies on a third-party website that can change without notice. Update the app, and if the problem continues, send us a bug report.

Reports and removal requests

DPGlance does not host or store anyone’s content. It shows what a profile already publishes publicly. If you are a creator and do not want your public profile to be visible, the quickest fix is to change your profile’s visibility in Snapchat. If you believe DPGlance is being used to harm someone or to infringe rights, send us a report using the form above, with the username concerned and what the problem is. We review every request and act where appropriate.
